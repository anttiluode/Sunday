## Interpretation

The Gate-8 anisotropy is not four unrelated accidents. Over the seven-dimensional controlled relation-program span, the finite-amplitude write/read behavior is extraordinarily close to a linear transformation.

A scalar identity/gain model fails badly, so the substrate is not merely preserving or amplifying the taught relation vector. An independent gain per physical pair also fails badly, so fixed pairwise ease/difficulty is insufficient. Cross-edge mixing is load-bearing.

Most of the fitted operator energy lies in three singular modes, and the preferred input subspace is reproducible across independently randomized substrates.

Safe statement:

> **Over the seven-dimensional controlled relation-program span, Sunday's fixed structural learning dynamics act approximately as a predictable linear write/read operator. About 98% of the fitted operator energy lies in three singular modes, and the preferred three-dimensional input subspace is stable across fresh random substrates.**

This explains Gate 8's apparent clean, diffuse and rotated codes as direction-dependent gain and cross-edge rotation by the substrate.

## What this does not establish

- The underlying material dynamics are not globally linear; Gate 9 concerns the finite training family and amplitude tested here.
- Rank three is not claimed universal outside this six-terminal controlled span.
- The operator is not yet derived from graph topology or the learning rule; it is measured empirically.
- Parameter efficiency is still poor versus an explicit small relation representation.
- Gate 7's rank-2/nonlinear endpoint compression attacker on an individual output matrix remains logically separate.
